Alfred – Briggsy Designs

A private, single-user office assistant for Briggsy Designs (QBCC licence 15506483), a building design practice on the Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia.

What Alfred is

Alfred is an internal automation tool used by Harry Briggs, the sole operator of Briggsy Designs, to manage his own business administration. It runs on a computer owned by Briggsy Designs and is not offered to the public, to clients, or to any other user.

What Alfred does with Google data

Alfred connects to Harry Briggs's own Google account (Gmail and Google Drive) in order to:

  • read incoming email to flag new client enquiries and payment notifications;
  • prepare draft replies and invoice follow-ups for Harry to review and send himself — Alfred never sends email;
  • read business files stored in Google Drive.

Alfred does not access any Google account other than Harry Briggs's own, and does not share Google user data with anyone.
